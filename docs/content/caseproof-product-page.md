## H2: How medical report digitisation works

1. **Intake:** Cases arrive automatically through a secure intake API, or your team uploads them in bulk.
2. **AI extraction:** AI extracts the data from every page and document type, queued and processed in parallel.
3. **Human QC:** Reviewers verify each report in a side-by-side QC workspace, with field-level corrections and full history.
4. **Delivery:** Results are delivered as structured data, branded PDF reports or Excel, or pushed straight into your systems.

## Copy notes for the team
1. **Spelling:** the copy uses "digitisation" (Indian/British). Keep it, but include "digitization" naturally in a couple of places such as the FAQ and alt text, since searchers use both.
2. **Images:** use synthetic or fully de-identified data in every screenshot. A real patient's report in a screenshot is a DPDP problem and a trust problem with insurers.
3. **Claims to verify:** on-premise option, hosting region, accuracy figure, turnaround SLA, results numbers, and that ECG/TMT extraction covers the fields listed.
4. **Accessibility:** descriptive alt text on screenshots, e.g. "Side-by-side QC view showing a scanned lab report and extracted test values".
